Adult Content Blocker

A lightweight Android app that blocks adult content on your device. Install it directly from the .apk below — no Play Store required.

Download APK

How to install

Follow these steps in order on your Android phone. It takes about five minutes.

1. Install the app

  1. Tap Download APK above. The file (AdultContentBlocker.apk) will land in your Downloads folder.
  2. Open your Files app, go to Downloads, and tap AdultContentBlocker.apk.
  1. Tap Install on the "Install this app?" prompt.
  1. Google Play Protect will scan the file and say "This app looks safe" — tap Install again to confirm.

Since this app isn't on the Play Store, Android shows extra warnings during this process. That's expected — Play Protect scans every .apk you install, and once it says the app looks safe you're good to continue.

2. Open the app and grant permissions

  1. Open the app from your home screen (look for the shield icon).
  1. When asked to allow notifications, tap Allow — this lets the app alert you when it blocks something.
  2. You'll land on a Setup screen with a few items to turn on, each with an Open settings button.

Accessibility service

  1. Tap Open settings next to "Accessibility service."
  2. Tap AntiAdultContent monitoring in the list.
  1. Turn on Use AntiAdultContent monitoring.
  2. Tap Allow on the "Allow full control of your device?" prompt. This is required so the app can detect which app or website is open and block adult content.

Display over other apps

  1. Back on the Setup screen, tap Open settings next to "Display over other apps," then tap AntiAdultContent in the list.
  1. Turn on Allow display over other apps. This lets the app show a full-screen block page when it catches something, instead of just a plain screen.

Ignore battery optimization

  1. Tap Open settings next to "Ignore battery optimization" and tap Allow. This stops Android from shutting the app down in the background, so protection stays active.

3. Set up Private DNS (recommended)

This is the most important step — it blocks adult content network-wide, at the DNS level, in a way apps can't bypass by redirecting around the app's own filtering.

  1. On the Setup screen, find Private DNS filtering and tap Set it up (or go to Settings → Network & Internet → Private DNS yourself).
  2. Select Private DNS provider hostname and enter: family-filter-dns.cleanbrowsing.org
  3. Tap Save.

You'll know it worked when Network and Internet → Private DNS shows "family-filter-dns.cleanbrowsing.org" underneath it, like this.

4. Choose what to protect

Back in the app, under Protection:

  1. Tap Choose blocked apps to select any apps you want blocked outright.
  2. Tap Choose monitored browsers and check every browser installed on your phone (e.g. Chrome, Kiwi) so their address bars get checked against the blocklist.
  3. Force SafeSearch is on by default — it adds SafeSearch to Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, and Brave in the browsers you monitor.

Once everything above shows a checkmark on the Setup screen, protection is active. Blocked attempts will show up under "Recent blocks" in the app.
