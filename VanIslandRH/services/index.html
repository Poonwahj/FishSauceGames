Diagnostic & Clinical Services

Comprehensive pulmonary diagnostic services available through Dr. Phang's clinic and the Royal Jubilee Hospital PFT Laboratory.

Spirometry

Spirometry is the most common pulmonary function test. You breathe into a mouthpiece connected to a spirometer, which measures how much air you can inhale and exhale, and how quickly. It is essential for diagnosing and monitoring asthma, COPD, and other lung conditions.

  • FEV1 and FVC measurement
  • Bronchodilator reversibility testing
  • Results interpreted against age/sex/height norms
  • Takes approximately 20–30 minutes

Full Pulmonary Function Tests

A complete PFT battery goes beyond spirometry to measure lung volumes, diffusion capacity (DLCO), and airway resistance. This provides a comprehensive picture of lung function for complex or unclear diagnoses.

  • Body plethysmography (lung volumes)
  • DLCO (gas transfer / diffusion)
  • Airway resistance (Raw)
  • Available at Royal Jubilee Hospital PFT Lab

Methacholine Challenge

When spirometry is normal but asthma is suspected, a methacholine challenge test can detect airway hyperresponsiveness. You inhale increasing concentrations of methacholine while spirometry is repeated to identify the threshold at which airflow decreases.

  • Performed when baseline spirometry is normal
  • Identifies airway hyperresponsiveness
  • High negative predictive value for asthma
  • Supervised by trained staff

Lung Imaging

Various imaging modalities provide complementary information about lung structure and disease.

  • Chest X-ray: initial evaluation of infection, effusion, masses
  • High-resolution CT (HRCT): ILD, emphysema, bronchiectasis
  • CT-guided lung biopsy: tissue sampling for suspected malignancy or ILD
  • PET/CT: staging of lung cancer

Bronchoscopy

Flexible bronchoscopy involves passing a thin, flexible scope through the nose or mouth into the airways to visualize the bronchial tree, collect samples, and perform therapeutic procedures.

  • Bronchoalveolar lavage (BAL) for infection and ILD
  • Endobronchial biopsies
  • EBUS (Endobronchial Ultrasound): staging lung cancer and sampling lymph nodes
  • Performed under sedation

Inhaler Education

Correct inhaler technique is essential for effective medication delivery. Studies show that up to 70–80% of patients use their inhalers incorrectly. We provide hands-on education for all inhaler device types.

  • Metered dose inhalers (MDI) with spacer
  • Turbuhaler® (Pulmicort, Symbicort)
  • Respimat® soft mist inhalers
  • Ellipta® and Breezhaler® devices

Airway Clearance

Airway clearance techniques help mobilize and clear mucus from the airways. These are particularly important for patients with bronchiectasis, cystic fibrosis, or COPD with excessive mucus production.

  • Active cycle of breathing techniques (ACBT)
  • Oscillating positive expiratory pressure (OPEP) devices
  • Autogenic drainage
  • Postural drainage and percussion
